What we build

Six façade systems, one accountable team

Every system below is engineered, fabricated and installed in-house or through vetted coordination — so one team stays accountable from drawing to handover.

01

Structural Glazing

Frameless and semi-frameless glass systems that hold sightlines clean while meeting wind-load and seismic requirements.

02

Curtain Wall Systems

Unitised and stick-built curtain walls sized for high-rise commercial and institutional envelopes.

03

ACP Cladding

Aluminium composite panel cladding for weather-tight, low-maintenance exteriors with clean joint lines.

04

Aluminium Doors & Windows

Thermally efficient door and window systems built to spec for residential and commercial openings.

05

Spider Glazing

Point-fixed glass façades using stainless steel spider fittings for uninterrupted glass expanses.

06

Skylights & Canopies

Weatherproof overhead glazing and canopy structures for atriums, lobbies and walkways.
